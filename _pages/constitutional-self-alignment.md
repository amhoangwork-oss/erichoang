*Work in progress*

I'm exploring whether a language model can improve its own alignment by critiquing its responses and learning from those critiques. This project is still in its infancy.

## Where the idea came from

The main inspirations were [Constitutional AI](https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback) and [On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18734). Constitutional AI got me interested in models learning from their own critiques. OPSD showed how the same model, given extra context, could act as a teacher for itself.

I want to bring those ideas together: could self-critique provide a useful teaching signal for on-policy self-distillation? I'm interested in how far that could take alignment without needing so much human supervision or RLHF.

## Why I'm working on it

I take recursive self-improvement seriously. If models become better at improving themselves, I want to know whether they can help with their own alignment too.
